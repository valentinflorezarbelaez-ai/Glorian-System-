## Functional Requirements (EARS)

### FR-1114 (Ubiquitous - Verifiable (t, n) Distributed Key Setup)

THE SYSTEM SHALL generate $(t, n)$ Shamir secret shares $s_i = f(i) \pmod q$ for a degree $t-1$ polynomial with group secret $s = f(0)$, computing individual participant verification keys $Y_i = s_i \cdot G$ and aggregate group public key $PK = s \cdot G$ on secp256k1 without revealing $s$ to any individual participant.

### FR-1115 (State-Driven - Round 1 Nonce Commitments & Anti-ROS Binding)

WHEN signers in active subset $S \subseteq \{1, \dots, n\}$ ($|S| \ge t$) enter signing Round 1, EACH participant $i \in S$ SHALL sample hiding nonce $d_i \in \mathbb{Z}_q^*$ and binding nonce $e_i \in \mathbb{Z}\_q^*$, publish commitments $(D_i = d_i \cdot G, E_i = e_i \cdot G)$, and evaluate binding factor $\rho_i = \text{SHA256}(\text{"FROST_RHO:"} \,\|\, i \,\|\, m \,\|\, \mathcal{B}) \pmod q$ where $\mathcal{B} = \{(j, D_j, E_j)\}_{j \in S}$.

### FR-1116 (Event-Driven - Group Commitment Aggregation & Challenge Derivation)

THE SYSTEM SHALL aggregate participant commitments into group commitment point $R = \sum_{i \in S} (D_i + \rho_i \cdot E_i)$, verify $R \ne \mathcal{O}$, and compute global Schnorr challenge $c = \text{SHA256}(\text{"FROST_CHALLENGE:"} \,\|\, R \,\|\, PK \,\|\, m) \pmod q$.

### FR-1117 (State-Driven - Round 2 Partial Signature Share Evaluation & Aggregation)

EACH participant $i \in S$ SHALL compute response share $z_i = (d_i + e_i \cdot \rho_i + \lambda_i \cdot s_i \cdot c) \pmod q$ where $\lambda_i = \prod_{j \in S, j \ne i} \frac{j}{j - i} \pmod q$ is the Lagrange interpolation coefficient. THE SYSTEM SHALL aggregate shares into final scalar $z = \sum_{i \in S} z_i \pmod q$, emitting standard Schnorr signature $\sigma = (R, z)$.

### FR-1118 (Ubiquitous - O(1) Standard Schnorr Verification & Frontier Dispatch)

THE SYSTEM SHALL verify $\sigma = (R, z)$ against group key $PK$ and message $m$ in constant $O(1)$ operations ($z \cdot G \stackrel{?}{=} R + c \cdot PK$) in $\le 2$ milliseconds, emit immutable SHA-256 receipts, and expose CLI commands `frost:keygen`, `frost:sign`, `frost:verify` and MCP actions `frost_keygen`, `frost_sign`, `frost_verify`.

## BDD Scenarios

### BDD-1114: Verifiable (t, n) Distributed Key Setup and Group Key Derivation

GIVEN threshold $t = 3$ and total signers $n = 5$
WHEN key generation setup is executed
THEN the system MUST derive 5 distinct participant shares $s_1 \dots s_5$
AND any subset of 3 or more shares MUST interpolate to the identical group public key $PK$.

### BDD-1115: Round 1 Nonce Commitments and Anti-ROS Attack Binding Factors

GIVEN signers $S = \{1, 2, 3\}$ signing message $m$
WHEN Round 1 commitments are exchanged
THEN each signer MUST emit unique $(D_i, E_i)$ commitments
AND binding factors $\rho_i$ MUST be uniquely bound to the specific participant, message, and commitment list $\mathcal{B}$.

### BDD-1116: Round 2 Threshold Signing and Lagrange Share Aggregation

GIVEN active signing subset $S = \{1, 3, 5\}$ ($|S| = 3 \ge t$)
WHEN each signer submits partial response share $z_i$
THEN individual shares MUST be verifiable against participant keys $Y_i$
AND the aggregated response $z = \sum_{i \in S} z_i \pmod q$ MUST combine with $R$ into valid signature $\sigma = (R, z)$.

### BDD-1117: Standard Single-Key Schnorr Verification Equivalence and Forgery Rejection

GIVEN a FROST signature $\sigma = (R, z)$ generated by a threshold swarm
WHEN verified by a standard Schnorr verifier with only group public key $PK$
THEN verification MUST return `VALID` in under 2 milliseconds
AND WHEN message $m$ is altered, or $z$ is modified by 1
THEN verification MUST return `INVALID` immediately.

### BDD-1118: Frontier CLI and MCP Dispatch

GIVEN the Glorian CLI and MCP control plane
WHEN `glorian frost:sign --message="tx-transfer-99" --json` is invoked
THEN the command MUST simulate threshold signing, verify in-memory, and output valid JSON with SHA-256 receipt.
