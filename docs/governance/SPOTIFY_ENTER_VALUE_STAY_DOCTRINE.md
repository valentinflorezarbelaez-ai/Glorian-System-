## Purpose

Operating bar to **enter Spotify**, become indispensable in any engineering seat, and build a long-term career — combining Spotify’s operational architecture with Glorian technical/human discipline.

## 1. Culture & operating model

### Squads · Tribes · Chapters · Guilds
- **Squad** (≈6–12): autonomous end-to-end ownership (design → prod → monitor).
- **Tribe**: product cluster (e.g. Music Expression, playback).
- **Chapters / Guilds**: cross-cutting craft communities (TypeScript/Web, Backend, AI, Audio DSP) — where quality standards and knowledge scale company-wide.
- **Stay signal:** do not stop at Squad delivery; contribute to Chapters/Guilds.

### Ownership mindset
- Compensation (RSUs / flexible equity participation) rewards **owner thinking**.
- Irreplaceable path: end-to-end problem ownership without step-by-step supervision.
