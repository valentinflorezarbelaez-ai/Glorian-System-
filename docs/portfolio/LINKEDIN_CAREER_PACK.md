> *"Why is immutability and bitemporality critical in high-reliability financial systems, and how do you design for it?"*

**Tu respuesta**:
> *"In distributed financial systems, in-place state mutation destroys auditability and leads to irreversible race conditions. Inspired by Nubank's use of Datomic, in Glorian I designed a bitemporal WAL ledger that models two orthogonal timelines: transaction time (when the system recorded the fact) and valid time (when the fact holds true in reality). This guarantees mathematical correctness rather than relying on wishful testing."*
